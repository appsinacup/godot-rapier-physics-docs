I've decided to stop maintaining and supporting Godot Rapier and my other Godot addons.

<!-- truncate -->

## Why

There are two reasons for this:

- **The Godot community's position on AI.** I use AI in my work. The community has recently made it clear they are against AI, for example with the [r/godot subreddit changes](https://www.reddit.com/r/godot/comments/1xy14d5/announcing_subreddit_changes/). You can also see how my own [announcement about AI usage in Godot Rapier](https://www.reddit.com/r/godot/comments/1xyrte5/about_the_future_of_godot_rapier_and_ai_usage/) was received. I had planned to keep maintaining the addons, but given all this, I won't anymore. The reason I started making these addons in the first place was the community.
- **Focus.** I want to put my time into my own products: my games, my game engine and my game server, rather than addons for someone else's engine.

## What happens to Godot Rapier?

**Godot Rapier v0.36.1 is the last release I will work on.** The project isn't going away, though. [Sébastien Crozet](https://github.com/sebcrozet), the creator of Rapier, will take over maintenance from here.

My other Godot addons, such as Whisper and Softbody2D, will no longer be maintained by me.

## Need help anyway?

If you rely on one of these addons and need a bugfix or support, I offer [paid consulting](https://appsinacup.com/consulting/) at **€50/hour**. I'll estimate the scope and time clearly before starting any work. Reach out directly if you're interested.

Thanks to everyone who used, tested and contributed to these projects. I hope you understand.
